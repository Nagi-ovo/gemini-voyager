# Image Refinement (Watermark Removal)

::: tip Safari Support
Since v1.6.0, **Image Refinement** works directly in Safari, including full-size watermark-free downloads. Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari now share the same built-in workflow.
:::

**AI Images, kept pure.**

Images generated by Gemini™ come with a visible watermark by default. While this is intended for safety, there are creative scenarios where you need a perfectly clean slate.

## Lossless Reconstruction

Image Refinement uses a **Reverse Alpha Blending** algorithm.

- **Not AI Inpainting**: Traditional watermark removal often uses AI to "smear" the area, which destroys pixel details.
- **Pixel Perfection**: We use mathematical calculations to precisely remove the transparent watermark layer, restoring the 100% original pixels.
- **Zero Quality Loss**: The processed image remains identical to the original in all non-watermarked areas.

## How to Use

Open the Voyager popup and turn on the rows you want under **Plugins**. Both are off by default:

- **Remove watermark on download** (Recommended): When you click Gemini's download button, the saved file has the watermark removed. A small 🍌 badge on the download button shows it's active; the button itself is still Gemini's own.
- **Remove watermark in preview** (marked **Experimental**): Also cleans the images shown on screen. It's slower, and an image may briefly flash while it's processed.

Turning download removal off leaves subsequent downloads untouched. A download already being processed finishes with the setting it started with, without saving a second copy because of the switch. Turning preview removal off restores the original images only where Voyager’s replacement is still displayed; images changed by Gemini afterward are left alone.

## Gemini's Built-in Option

Gemini is rolling out its own visible-watermark switch: **Settings → Media watermark → Off**. If your account has it, use it and turn off Voyager's watermark removal; Gemini's option is more reliable. Voyager shows a one-time notice about it, and tells you when your recent images already come without the watermark. If you can't find the switch yet, keep using Voyager. SynthID and C2PA metadata remain either way.

## Privacy & Security

All processing happens **locally in your browser**. Your images are never uploaded to any third-party servers, ensuring your privacy and creative security.
