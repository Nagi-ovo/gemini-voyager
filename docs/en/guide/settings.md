# Make It Yours

The default experience is great. But you might want it perfect.
Customize every pixel.

## Cinema Mode

Why view the future through a tiny keyhole?
Voyager lets you expand the chat width.

- **Chat width**: In the popup, turn on **Page layout** → **Chat Width** first. It's off by default, and the slider shows under the row once it's on. The slider then goes from 30% to 100% of the window (70% by default). Go wide for code and complex tables, narrow for reading.
- **Edit input width**: Turn on **Page layout** → **Edit input width** first, then widen the box you get when editing a sent message.

## Control

Click the extension icon to access the control center.

- **Scroll mode**: In **Timeline** → **Show Timeline**, `Flow` scrolls smoothly to the message; `Jump` goes there instantly.
- **Timeline position**: In **Timeline** → **Show Timeline**, turn on **Draggable Timeline** and move it where it feels right. **Reset Timeline Position** puts it back.
- **Visual Effects**: On Gemini and AI Studio, switch on **Page layout** → **Visual effects**, then pick `Snow`, `Sakura`, or `Rain` under the row; switch it off to turn the effect off. On other sites (Claude, ChatGPT, DeepSeek, and custom sites) the same row sits in that site's **Plugins** card, once Voyager runs there (Prompt Manager or a plugin turned on for the site).

## Custom Order

Too many sections in the popup, and the ones you use most are buried at the bottom?

Hover over any settings card and ▲/▼ arrows appear in the top-right corner. Click to move a card up or down. Your layout is saved automatically and persists across sessions.

Features are grouped into topic cards (Page layout, Sidebar, Timeline, Answer content, and so on), and a feature's options show right under its switch once it is on. The search box and the row of card names under it stay at the top while you scroll; click a name to jump to that card. Flipped a switch by mistake? Press **Undo** in the message at the bottom.

## Atmosphere

Voyager is not limited to utility tweaks. You can also change the mood of the page.

- **Snow**: Soft drifting flakes for a calm winter feel.
- **Sakura**: Floating cherry blossom petals for a lighter spring vibe.
- **Rain**: A cinematic rain overlay with angled streaks and subtle splash ripples.
- **Smooth switching**: Turning an effect off or switching to another one lets existing particles fade out naturally.
