# Make It Yours

The default experience is great. But you might want it perfect.
Customize every pixel.

## Cinema Mode

Why view the future through a tiny keyhole?
Voyager lets you expand the chat width.

- **Chat width**: Turn on the switch next to **Chat width** first. It's off by default, and the slider stays collapsed until you do. The slider then goes from 30% to 100% of the window (70% by default). Go wide for code and complex tables, narrow for reading.
- **Edit input width**: Turn on its switch first, then widen the box you get when editing a sent message.

## Control

Click the extension icon to access the control center.

- **Scroll mode**: `Flow` scrolls smoothly to the message; `Jump` goes there instantly.
- **Timeline position**: Turn on **Draggable Timeline** and move it where it feels right. **Reset Timeline Position** puts it back.
- **Visual Effects**: Pick `Snow`, `Sakura`, or `Rain` for a seasonal atmosphere.

## Custom Order

Too many sections in the popup, and the ones you use most are buried at the bottom?

Hover over any settings card and ▲/▼ arrows appear in the top-right corner. Click to move a card up or down. Your layout is saved automatically and persists across sessions.

## Atmosphere

Voyager is not limited to utility tweaks. You can also change the mood of the page.

- **Snow**: Soft drifting flakes for a calm winter feel.
- **Sakura**: Floating cherry blossom petals for a lighter spring vibe.
- **Rain**: A cinematic rain overlay with angled streaks and subtle splash ripples.
- **Smooth switching**: Turning an effect off or switching to another one lets existing particles fade out naturally.
