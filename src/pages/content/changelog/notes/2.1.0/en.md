### Highlights

- 🌉 **:chatgpt: ChatGPT Local Bridge** _(off by default)_: lets programs on your computer bridge to ChatGPT on the web, with primitives to open a chat, send a prompt with files, wait for and read the reply, and manage project files. Reference usage: [Voyager CLI](https://github.com/voyager-crew/voyager-cli), for the terminal and coding agents.
- ⭕ **:chatgpt: ChatGPT Pro usage this week** _(off by default)_: a small ring next to the model picker shows how much GPT-6 Pro you've used this week; hover it for the count. The limit follows your plan, Pro Lite or Pro. In Chrome and Edge.

### Fixes

- **:gemini: Gemini hidden archived chats**: the sidebar keeps loading older chats even when a whole newly loaded page is filed in folders.
- **:gemini: Gemini with several accounts**: an email address in a reply is no longer taken for the signed-in account, so folders no longer load under the wrong one.
- **:chatgpt: ChatGPT reply notifications**: clicking Work mode's project or files button no longer silences the notification.
- **Export**: a reply with a table no longer exports as only the table, losing the headings, paragraphs and lists around it.
