### Highlights

- 🧩 **Write your own plugins**: describe a change in one sentence in the popup's local plugins card, let any AI write it, and Voyager checks the reply and shows what it will change before importing it (turned off). A coding agent can write one too. [→ Docs](/guide/write-a-plugin)
- 📁 **:chatgpt: ChatGPT folders**: file chats into folders inside ChatGPT's own sidebar, with nesting, drag to reorder, multi-select, `f:` search, Activity view and cloud sync.
- 🔌 **21 new official plugins** for :chatgpt: ChatGPT, :claude: Claude and :deepseek: DeepSeek, from a ChatGPT timeline to quote reply, draft auto-save and table copy. All start turned off.
- 📚 **Saved Library page**: starred messages and highlights open in their own tab, with the same filters, search and cards as the popup.

### New

- **:chatgpt: ChatGPT timeline** _(off by default)_: jump between your prompts and star them, as on Gemini and Claude.
- **Quote reply** _(off by default)_: select text in a ChatGPT, Claude or DeepSeek answer and quote it into your draft.
- **Ctrl/⌘+Enter to send** _(off by default)_: Enter adds a new line on ChatGPT, Claude and DeepSeek; Ctrl/⌘+Enter sends.
- **Draft auto-save** _(off by default)_: unsent text on :claude: Claude and :deepseek: DeepSeek is kept on this device and comes back after a refresh.
- **Draft counter** _(off by default)_: a live character, word or line count above the message box on ChatGPT, Claude and DeepSeek.
- **Reading typography** _(off by default)_: answer text size, line height and paragraph spacing on ChatGPT, Claude and DeepSeek.
- **:deepseek: DeepSeek extras** _(off by default)_: copy tables as Markdown or TSV, fold long code blocks, and copy the thinking section.
- **:chatgpt: ChatGPT extras** _(off by default)_: ECharts and WaveDrom diagrams, collapsible long messages, and LaTeX in your own messages.
- **Research Pack** _(off by default)_: gather :gemini: Gemini answers with their prompts and sources into one Markdown pack, then copy it, download it or continue in ChatGPT or Claude.
- **More cloud sync**: ChatGPT folders sync on their own, and the timeline outlines and stars of ChatGPT, Claude and DeepSeek now back up to Google Drive.
- **Timelines everywhere match Gemini**: ChatGPT, Claude and DeepSeek timelines get the Nodes, Compact and Ruler styles, shortcuts and previews, set from the popup.
- **Starred prompts keep their full text**: cards expand to show it, and search covers it.
- **Broken-feature notice**: when a :gemini: Gemini update breaks a Voyager feature, the popup says so and offers a privacy-safe report link.
- **One look for notices**: toasts and confirmations share one stacked, themed design instead of browser alerts.
- **:gemini: Gemini watermark switch**: removal now starts off on new installs. If you're updating, your current setting stays.

### Fixes

- **Unstarred stays unstarred**: a message you unstarred no longer comes back after cloud sync.
- **:chatgpt: ChatGPT export**: long chats export in full again, with progress and Cancel, and code blocks export as code.
- **:gemini: Gemini sidebar**: loading older chats no longer stalls with folders and draft auto-save on (#1040).
- **Safer folders**: several open tabs no longer overwrite each other's folder changes, and a failed read can't wipe your folders.
- **Prompt library**: edits in several tabs, imports and Drive merges keep your order, pins and latest edits.
- **Default model**: works with Gemini's Low, Medium and High thinking options.
- **Markdown export**: keeps every image instead of stopping at 40.
- **Opening saved stars**: starred ChatGPT and DeepSeek messages open on their own site.
- **:chatgpt: ChatGPT theme**: Voyager follows ChatGPT's light or dark theme instead of the system's.
- **After an update**: open tabs no longer show doubled Voyager panels.
- **:aistudio: AI Studio**: deleted folders stay deleted, and folder actions no longer reload the page.
- **Drive restore**: conversation forks are restored too.
- **:gemini: Gemini forks**: opening a fork no longer reloads the page.
- **Response completion notifications**: turning them on now sticks after you answer the browser's permission prompt.
